## Key Legal Points

### 1. Referral Nature
- We receive referral fees (3-8%) from contractors for successful matches
- We do not mark up contractor pricing
- We do not guarantee contractor performance

### 2. Contractor Independence
- All contractors are independent businesses
- We verify licenses and references, but do not guarantee work quality
- Contractual relationships are between investor and contractor directly

### 3. Foreign Investment (Fideicomiso)
- Mexican law (Article 27 of the Constitution) restricts foreign ownership within 50km of coasts
- A bank trust (fideicomiso) is required for foreign property ownership in this zone
- We provide educational guidance only — not legal or financial advice
- All transactions must involve a licensed Mexican notario público

### 4. Social Purpose Company
- Structured as S.A.P.I. de C.V. with social purpose statutes
- Commitments: tree planting, carbon offsets, community reinvestment
- Annual social impact report published publicly

### 5. Privacy & Data
- Lead data stored in PostgreSQL with industry-standard encryption in transit
- No data sold to third parties
- Data shared only with matched contractors for the specific project
- Right to deletion available upon request (GDPR/LFPDPPP compliance)

### 6. No Professional Advice
- Information on this website does not constitute legal, financial, tax, or construction advice
- Consult licensed professionals in Mexico for all legal and financial matters
- Exchange rates and pricing are estimates only

## Regulatory Requirements
- **RFC**: Required for all Mexican business operations
- **REPSE**: Required for outsourcing/referral services (per 2021 labor reform)
- **IMSS**: Required if hiring employees
- **SAT**: Monthly tax declarations, annual audit
