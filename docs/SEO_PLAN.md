## Page Strategy

### Home Page
- Target: brand + "construcción puerto vallarta"
- Schema.org: LocalBusiness
- H1: "Construcción Profesional en Puerto Vallarta"

### Geo Landing Pages (/zona/[slug])
- 13 pages, each targeting "[zone] + construction/contractor"
- Unique content per zone: market overview, typical projects, accessibility
- Schema.org: Service + Place
- Internal linking between zones

### Blog / Content Hub
- Target: informational + long-tail queries
- Topics:
  1. Fideicomiso guide (legal)
  2. Construction costs breakdown (pricing)
  3. Building permits process (guides)
  4. Best investment zones (investment)
  5. Rainy season construction tips (seasonal)
  6. Choosing a contractor in Mexico (guides)
  7. Luxury villa design trends (inspiration)
  8. Eco-friendly construction in Nayarit (sustainability)

### Services Page
- Target: service-specific keywords
- 6 sections with unique descriptions per service type
